Routing

Pace routing spends each account’s 7-day meter by its reset. Warm chats stay on their account. Cold chats go first to an account that is due: one whose measured 7-day balance your measured spend per hour needs about the time to its reset to use. Otherwise they go to the account that must be spent fastest. Forced moves preserve the client’s compaction checkpoint.
